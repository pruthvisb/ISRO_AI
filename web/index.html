The Scientific Discovery Pipeline

How raw stellar data is ingested, cleaned, analyzed, and classified by the AstroPulse AI engine.

1. Raw Data Ingestion

Queries TESS Input Catalog (TIC) & MAST Archive to retrieve light curve fluxes.

2A. Stellar Processing

Removes NaNs, clips outliers at 3σ, and fits a Savitzky-Golay trend to flatten pulsations.

2B. Orbit Detection

Runs Box Least Squares (BLS) period search and phase-folds light curves relative to t0.

3. Feature Engineering

Extracts statistical moments, odd-even differences, and creates 200-bin folded profiles.

4A. Tabular Ensemble

Trains Random Forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM models on statistical metadata.

4B. Deep Learning

Deploys PyTorch 1D CNN and CNN-LSTM models on phase-folded vectors.

5. Ensemble Consensus

Averages probabilities from all five models to calculate the final exoplanet confidence score (0-100%).

Transit Depth Equation

$$\delta = \left(\frac{R_p}{R_*}\right)^2$$

Calculates fractional dip in stellar brightness based on the planet/star area ratio. Live: 1.06%

Kepler's Third Law

$$a = \left(M_* \cdot P_{\text{yr}}^2\right)^{1/3}$$

Computes orbital distance a in AU from the period P and stellar mass M*. Live: 0.053 AU

Equilibrium Temperature

$$T_{\text{eq}} = T_* (1-A)^{1/4} \sqrt{\frac{R_*}{2a}}$$

Estimates planet surface temperature assuming Bond albedo A = 0.3. Live: 279 K

Machine Learning Architecture

Deploying a diverse ensemble of models designed for both tabular metadata and sequence-based phase profiles.

Tabular

Random Forest

Constructs an ensemble of decision trees over features like skewness, kurtosis, and out-of-transit noise level, providing high interpretability and feature importance rankings.

Tabular

XGBoost

Uses gradient-boosted trees to handle complex non-linear feature relationships, excelling at separating eclipsing binaries from deep planet candidates.

Tabular

LightGBM

Leaf-wise tree growth enables extremely fast training and predictions, acting as the speed-focused vetting classifier in the tabular ensemble.

Deep Learning

1D CNN

Ingests the binned, phase-folded light curve phase vector. Convolutional layers act as spatial shape matchers to identify U-shaped transits and V-shaped eclipses directly.

Deep Learning

CNN-LSTM Hybrid

Pairs feature-extracting 1D CNNs with a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) layer to capture sequential dependencies in phase space, excelling on low SNR signals.
